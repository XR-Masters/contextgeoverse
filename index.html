Spatial Commerce Engine

The world's first location-based recommendation marketplace — connecting visitors to the right businesses at the right moment, driven by AI that understands where they are, how much time they have, and what they actually want.

How the recommendation engine works

Every visitor carries signals — their location within the venue, how long they have been there, how much time remains, their stated preferences, and their budget intent. The engine synthesises all of these in real time to deliver genuinely useful recommendations, not generic ads.

Visitors opt in to personalisation and can set spending budgets and category preferences from within the app. The engine respects these constraints — making every recommendation feel like advice from a trusted local guide.

01 Location — where the visitor is right now, down to zone within the venue
02 Time — how long they have been here and how much visit time remains
03 Preference — stated interests and category settings configured by the visitor
04 Budget — optional spending intent the visitor sets — daily limits, per-category caps
05 Context — nearby businesses that are relevant, available, and bidding for this placement right now

A two-sided marketplace

The engine serves two audiences simultaneously. For visitors — personalised, trustworthy spatial guidance. For local businesses — a direct channel to reach the right person at the moment they are physically closest and most likely to act.

Visitor Side

Smart spatial guidance

Personalised recommendations delivered via mobile AR or AI glasses. Budget controls, preference settings, and opt-in data — full transparency and control for the visitor at all times.

Business Side

AI-powered onboarding

Local businesses sign up through a self-serve AI onboarding flow. No sales team required for standard accounts. Pay only for the results you choose — lead, action, view, or subscription.

Privacy by design

All visitor data is opt-in, GDPR-compliant, and processed under explicit consent. Budget and preference controls are visitor-owned. This is a design requirement, not a compliance checkbox — and a core selling point for municipalities and public sector clients.

For Local Businesses

Five flexible revenue models let any local business — from an independent café to a hotel group — participate in the spatial commerce network at exactly the risk and commitment level that suits them.

CPL
Cost Per Lead

Pay only when a visitor is referred to your business. Zero upfront cost. The lowest-risk entry into the spatial commerce network for independent operators and SMBs.

CPA
Cost Per Action

Pay when a referred visitor completes a defined action — a booking, purchase, or redemption. Higher cost-per-unit, highest confidence in ROI. Best for conversion-focused campaigns.

CPV
Per View

Pay for spatial impressions — contextual AR brand placement delivered to relevant visitors in your area. Suited to awareness campaigns, hotel chains, and large retail brands.

SUB
Subscription

Fixed monthly presence in the spatial network — guaranteed placement within your category and location zone. Best value for established anchor businesses with steady footfall.

SPO
Sponsorship

Premium venue-level or zone-level sponsorship — exclusive spatial branding within defined areas. Suited to tourism boards, government authorities, and major brand partners.
