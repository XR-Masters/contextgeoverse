Section 01

Program Overview & Vision

The OSCP Certification Program is the world's first professional certification in Open Spatial Computing, issued by the Open AR Cloud Association (OARC). It establishes a globally recognized credential framework for individuals who understand, build upon, and contribute to the open spatial web.

Spatial computing is transitioning from experimental to infrastructural — AI glasses, spatial browsers, and location-anchored digital experiences are becoming mainstream. The OSCP Certification Program exists to train and credentialize the practitioners who will build this future using open, interoperable standards rather than proprietary silos.

The program is structured in three progressive levels, each building on the last, and maps directly to the technology stack defined in the Open Spatial Computing Platform (OSCP) specification maintained by OARC.

Standards basis: All three levels are aligned with OGC GeoPose, OSCP protocol specifications, and the Metaverse Standards Forum (MSF) Real/Virtual World Integration Working Group guidelines. Certified practitioners are recognized as standards-literate contributors to the open spatial ecosystem.

Target Audience

The program is designed for a broad audience at different points in their spatial computing journey:

  • Students & educators — high school through university, especially STEM and computer science programs
  • Developers & engineers — building location-based AR/XR applications
  • Architects & platform leads — designing interoperable spatial systems at scale
  • Industry professionals — in tourism, urban planning, smart cities, retail, and education transitioning into spatial computing roles
  • Researchers & academics — working in ubiquitous computing, HCI, geospatial systems, and related fields

Issuing Authority

All certificates are issued exclusively by the Open AR Cloud Association (OARC) — the global non-profit driving open, interoperable AR Cloud technology. Delivery partners (including XR Masters / MyGeoVerse and Augmented City) may co-deliver curriculum but do not appear as co-issuers on certificates. This ensures consistent global credibility and standards independence.

Section 03

Level 1 — OSCP Foundations (OSCP-F)

The Foundations level is the entry point to the OSCP Certification Program. It is derived from the full OARC tutorial delivered at UbiComp ISWC 2025 (Aalto University, Finland) and expanded to a complete 4-hour single-day format. It is designed to be accessible to anyone — no programming or AR experience required.

Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the spatial web vision and distinguish it from existing AR/VR approaches
  • Describe the role and structure of the Open AR Cloud and OSCP platform
  • Define OGC GeoPose and explain why a universal pose reference standard matters
  • Explain how Visual Positioning Systems (VPS) work and name at least two implementations
  • Navigate the MyGeoVerse platform and deploy a geolocated AR object
  • Describe the difference between GPS-level and VPS-level localization accuracy
  • Demonstrate awareness of interoperability challenges in current spatial ecosystems

Assessment Requirements — Level 1 (OSCP-F)

  • Part A — Knowledge Quiz: 20 questions (multiple choice + 2 short answer). Passing score: 70%. Administered digitally at end of session.
  • Part B — Practical Demo: Submit screenshot or 30-second screen recording of a deployed, geolocated AR experience. Must include visible GPS coordinates confirming real-world placement.
  • Certificate issued: Within 5 business days of passing both parts. Delivered digitally with a unique credential ID and OARC verification link.
  • Retake policy: One free retake per part within 30 days. Subsequent retakes: $25 admin fee.

Level 2 — OSCP Builder (OSCP-B)

The Builder level takes practitioners from awareness to production. Participants build fully functional, standards-compliant spatial experiences from scratch — integrating VPS, implementing GeoPose, working with the spARcl web client, and deploying real AR Cloud applications. This level is currently in curriculum design; the framework below defines intended scope.

Assessment Requirements — Level 2 (OSCP-B)

  • Part A — Project Submission: Complete, deployed AR Cloud application with source code, documentation, and live demo. Must use at least one OSCP standard (GeoPose or OSCP-SSD).
  • Part B — Oral Review: 20-minute technical interview with two OARC-certified reviewers. Assesses depth of understanding and standards rationale.
  • Passing threshold: Project: 75% rubric score. Oral: Pass/Fail by consensus of both reviewers.
  • Prerequisite: Valid OSCP-F certificate (not expired).
Section 05

Level 3 — OSCP Architect (OSCP-A)

The Architect level is the expert credential in open spatial computing. It is designed for senior practitioners who are ready to design, lead, and contribute to the open spatial ecosystem at scale — including platform architecture, standards contribution, and ecosystem development. Curriculum is planned; the framework below defines intended scope.

Assessment Requirements — Level 3 (OSCP-A)

  • Part A — Architecture Thesis: A 20–30 page spatial computing architecture document covering a real deployment scenario. Must include standards compliance mapping, VPS strategy, and integration architecture.
  • Part B — Peer Review Panel: 45-minute defense before a panel of three OARC-certified Architects or OARC board members. Panel votes Pass/Fail by majority.
  • Additional requirement: Demonstrate active contribution to the spatial computing ecosystem (open-source contribution, published research, or standards body participation).
  • Prerequisite: Valid OSCP-B + at least one completed production deployment as lead developer.

Certificate Format

All certificates are issued digitally and include:

  • Recipient full name and certification level
  • Unique credential ID (format: OSCP-F-2026-XXXXX)
  • Issue date and expiry date
  • OARC seal and authorized signature
  • QR code linking to OARC's public verification registry
  • Standards bodies recognized (OGC GeoPose, OSCP, MSF)

A physical certificate print option will be available for Level 3 (OSCP-A) holders.

Verification Registry

OARC will maintain a public-facing verification registry at openarcloud.org/verify where any third party can confirm the validity and level of a certificate using the credential ID or QR code. This ensures employer and institution trust without requiring applicants to share documents.

Delivery Formats

FormatLevelsDescription
In-Person Workshop L1 L2 L3 Delivered at schools, universities, conferences, and OARC partner events. Instructor-led with live demos. Recommended for L1 at high school / university level.
Online Live (Synchronous) L1 L2 Instructor-led via video conference. Hands-on portions completed remotely using cloud-based tools. Assessment administered via digital platform.
Self-Paced Online L1 Pre-recorded video modules + interactive exercises. Quiz administered upon completion. Demo submission required. Available via OARC learning portal (planned).

School Testbed Program L1 Delivered through the OARC/MGV/Augmented City Joint Testbed Program. Integrated into the 3-week build sprint. L1 certificate issued at sprint completion.

Authorized Delivery Partners

Organizations wishing to deliver OSCP Certification content must be authorized by OARC. Delivery partner requirements:

  • At least one certified OSCP-B holder (for L1 delivery) or OSCP-A holder (for L2/L3 delivery) on the delivery team
  • Use of OARC-approved curriculum materials (slides, exercises, assessment questions)
  • Submit participant assessment results to OARC for certificate issuance
  • Annual delivery partner review and renewal

Current authorized delivery partners: XR Masters / MyGeoVerse (L1, L2), Augmented City (L1 VPS modules).
